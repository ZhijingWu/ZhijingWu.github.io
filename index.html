About

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at Sichuan University .

My research interests center on LLM agents, especially how agents plan, remember, recover from failures, and reason over structured environments. I am particularly interested in agent reliability and evaluation, long-horizon planning and memory, and multi-agent systems.

Graph machine learning provides a methodological foundation for my work on structured state, memory, and reasoning. I am also interested in language-model training and systems as supporting tools for agent research.
