Research & engineering

About Me

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Sichuan University. My research interests lie in embodied AI, world models, and intelligent agents, with a particular focus on how autonomous systems perceive, reason, and plan in complex environments.

I am interested in developing reliable and generalizable AI systems by combining structured reasoning, predictive modeling, and learning-based decision-making. I enjoy exploring fundamental research questions through both theoretical understanding and practical experimentation, and I am keen to pursue long-term research collaborations in these areas.

UAV Inspection Planner

Robotics · Path Planning · Engineering

Contributed topology-aware mission planning and start/end replanning to a team’s powerline-inspection prototype, integrating inspection workflows, visualization and tests. The system operates at mission-planning and simulation level.

EgoWM Planning Audit

World Models · Planning · Evaluation

Independently audited action-ranking sensitivity and coupled sampling in an existing action-conditioned world model. The variance-reduction target was not met; navigation success was not evaluated.

GitHub ↗

Dynamic Web-Agent Reliability

LLM Agents · Feedback · Reliability

Studied observation and action reliability in one controlled browser UI. Fresh observations prevented the tested stale actions, while later retries could undo an achieved goal.

GitHub ↗

AgentHijack Timing Extension

Computer Use · Agent Safety · Evaluation

Extended the upstream AgentHijack evaluation with within-step corruption timing controls. A descriptive study of three Chrome tasks examined how stale observations affect execution, with uncontrolled sampling limiting inference.

GitHub ↗ Research Note

CPC Memory Mechanism Diagnostic

Agent Memory · State Representation · Reasoning

Used symbolic controls across ten archived worlds to distinguish evidence retention, reader behavior and observational ambiguity. This exploratory mechanism study is separate from the ExpeL/WebShop audit.

GitHub ↗

GTD Execution-Semantics Audit

Multi-Agent Systems · Structured Communication

Independently reproduced the released GTD pipeline and audited communication-graph execution on the fork’s reproduction branch. Coordinate alignment improved graph fidelity without establishing downstream task-performance gains.
