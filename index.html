Selected Projects / Research

Diagnostic / Sensitivity Study

Communication-Cost Sensitivity in LLM Multi-Agent Protocol Assignment

Independent Study on ProtocolBench

I audited the released ProtocolBench / AgentProtocols GAIA routing path to ask whether workflow communication demand and measured runtime costs can change a fixed local protocol assignment.

I profiled A2A, ACP, ANP, and Agora over real local request paths with 1–100 KB payloads; 480/480 measured requests succeeded. Under controlled communication demand and official workflow configuration templates, I found an A2A → ANP assignment reversal. The eligible reversal persisted in a no-Agora ablation after separating forced candidate-set changes.

This diagnostic examines assignment sensitivity; it does not establish GAIA or ProtocolRouter improvement or general protocol superiority.

Reproduction / Execution-Semantics Audit

Execution-Semantics Audit of Guided Topology Diffusion

Multi-Agent Systems Reproduction Study

I reproduced the released GTD pipeline on GSM8K and traced generated communication graphs through diffusion supervision, runtime construction, execution, and final aggregation.

Across 603 records, raw-to-executed edge Jaccard was 0.152. A controlled canonical-coordinate adapter increased it to 0.863.

Independent Extension / Agent Evaluation

Within-Step Corruption Timing for Computer-Use Agents

Independent Extension of AgentHijack

I studied pre-observation, post-observation/pre-execution, and post-execution corruption timing across 3 Chrome tasks and 36 primary runs.

Post-observation/pre-execution corruption increased mean episode length relative to pre-observation on all 3/3 retained tasks: +4.00, +1.75, and +1.00 steps. One task also showed lower success under stale timing.

A descriptive controlled study with a small task set.
